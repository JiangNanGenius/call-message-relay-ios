# CallRelay icon

Generated and refined using the built-in imagegen tool, then exported at
1024 × 1024 for the Apple asset catalog. Original imagegen PNGs are retained in
the generation library. The app uses these opaque system-appearance assets:

- `CallRelay/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`
- `CallRelay/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon-Dark.png`

The approved abstract relay mark was refined to make its blue receiver and
cyan message bubble recognizable while retaining a single flowing silhouette.
iOS applies the icon mask and chooses the supplied appearance variant.
